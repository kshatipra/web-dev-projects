BEHIND THE RECOMMENDATION

Clear logic. No mystery score.

Each activity starts with its own comfortable temperature range and wind threshold. Your optional preferences override the preferred temperature range and adjust rain/wind penalties. Scores start at 100 and lose points for uncomfortable temperatures, higher precipitation probability, and wind above the activity threshold. All three penalties are shown for the top recommendation.

Weather data: OpenWeatherMap current weather + 5-day / 3-hour forecast. Recommendations use forecast data, not live road conditions or air quality. Route comparisons additionally use OSRM estimated driving routes, without traffic.
